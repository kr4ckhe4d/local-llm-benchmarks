One line each: date, task, decision, why. Append only.

- 2026-11-25 T01: default seed 42, default time 10h, default 800 petals, ACES tone mapping — simple fixed defaults, all overridable by URL params.

- 2026-11-25 T02: added tools/dbg.mjs (in-page JS eval via CDP) — shot.mjs can't answer pixel-level debug questions and is off-limits to edit.
- 2026-11-25 T02: day schedule 05:30–18:30, 65° max elevation, sun arcs +x → −z → −x — so review preset 18.5 lands at golden hour and 23h is deep night.

- 2026-11-25 T02: night is moonlit not lamp-lit — moonLight 3.2 × 0xaac2ff + hemi 0.3 × 0x2c4368 + fog 0x1e2c4a; lanterns (T07) and fireflies (T11) will carry the rest.
- 2026-11-25 T02: moon disc gets a −109° azimuth offset from the anti-sun so it is not always behind the overview camera; at t=23 it is above the overview frame top (+53° el vs +4° frame top) so the night view shows stars, not the disc — acceptable, acceptance only requires moon light + stars.
- 2026-11-25 T02: accepted brightPct 14–20 in pond/pagoda/underside day views — the physical sky is white at the horizon and those cameras frame it; overview-day 3.1 / torii 9.3 pass; real geometry will frame the horizon later.
- 2026-11-25 T02: exposure settled at 0.38 with sun 2.3 / hemi 0.3+0.7·dayF / envIntensity 0.45 — day not blown, sunset warm, night readable.
- 2026-11-25 T03: island textures are procedural canvas (grass/soil/rock strata + generated normal maps), not CC0 downloads — same visual class, zero asset risk, deterministic; CC0 downloads still allowed later if a pass wants richer maps.
- 2026-11-25 T03: terrain noise fades out at the rim (e>0.75..0.93) so the edge is a deterministic smooth circle; the underside's noisy rock lip pokes just OUTSIDE+ABOVE that exact edge to seal the solid — noisy terrain edge + separate underside left provable sky-slit holes (found via 2376-ray CDP test).
- 2026-11-25 T03: vertex colours on textured meshes must be BRIGHT MODULATIONS (~0.7–1.3), not full albedos — texture×vertex both in linear space double-darkened and the island rendered near-black.
- 2026-11-25 T04: pagoda geometry is merged per material (6 meshes: stone/plaster/timber/dark/tile/gold) — one draw call per material keeps it at ~6 calls instead of hundreds of boxes.
- 2026-11-25 T04: terrain is flattened in-place at the pagoda site (blend r 6..13 to h(0,−14), recomputeVertexNormals); island.terrainHeight() is NOT updated (pure function) — only matters for placements near the site; pond (z≈+12) is unaffected.
- 2026-11-25 T04: tokyō bracket sets are simplified boxes (3 per side per tier) and the roof is a 4-patch square frustum (t^1.6 width / t^1.8 height profiles, |u|^2.6·t^2.1 corner lift) — parametric enough to read as a pagoda at review distances, no per-bracket carving.
- 2026-11-25 T05: three Water addon (like Reflector) derives its mirror plane from the MESH transform (local +z = normal), NOT the vertex data — the fan must be built in the local XY plane with position+rotation on the mesh; baked y=2.55 + identity mesh made the 'mirror' a vertical plane and produced zero visible reflection (previous session's render-diff 'verification' hit stone/terrain pixels, false positive).
- 2026-11-25 T07: bridge crosses the pond at x=−2.8 (west of the koi cluster, r≤2.4 around (0,12)) so the koi stay clear in the pond view — verified 0% red-pixel overlap around all 6 koi.
- 2026-11-25 T07: `island.terrainHeight()` is a pure function that ignores in-place terrain edits (pond basin, pagoda/torii flattening) — `src/garden.js` adds a build-time `groundY(x,z)` raycast against `island.terrain` for lanterns and path stones; without it the torii-flattened patch left lantern L1 buried 0.49u and path stones near the torii floating 0.33u.
- 2026-11-25 T07: lantern night lights are capped at 2 PointLights (5 lights total) with emissive cores on all 4 lanterns — enough for the night read now, and bloom (T12) will sell the glow later.
- 2026-11-25 T08: blossom crowns are flat-shaded icosahedron puffs (no alpha cards) — cheap, correct shadows, no transparency sorting; "translucency feel" faked via pale pink albedo + slight warm emissive.
- 2026-11-25 T08: blossom palette deepened (0xeec7d6..0xdf85ac) after first pass — at exposure 0.38/ACES, pale 0xffd9e6 pinks washed to near-white (246,248,249) and the crowns lost their sakura read; mid pinks survive highlight desaturation.
- 2026-11-25 T08: wind sway is a vertex-shader `begin_vertex` injection (per-instance world phase, amplitude ∝ height above 1.5u, uWind from URL param, clock frozen when paused) — zero CPU cost; shadow maps stay static (sway ≤0.2u, imperceptible in shadows).
- 2026-11-25 T09: outside the in-place-edited zones (pond basin, pagoda/torii flattenings — all in the exclusion list) `island.terrainHeight()` is exact, so grass/moss/rocks scatter uses it directly instead of 13k raycasts (build-time cost).
- 2026-11-25 T09: circle at (11,3) r4.3 is reserved in the vegetation exclusion list for the T10 zen gravel bed — keeps that area free of dense grass so T10 only needs to place the gravel surface, not re-scatter.
- 2026-11-25 T09: grass casts no shadows (alphaTest cards would cast solid-quad shadows without a custom depth material); 13k 0.3–0.6u blades barely read in shadow maps anyway.
- 2026-11-25 T09: hand-rolled hash2 for meadow noise lost high bits (`(h ^ h>>13) * CONST` without 32-bit wrap) → values bunched ≤0.48, 93% of scatter rejected (3205/13000 grass); fixed with `|0` wraps (mulberry-style).
- 2026-11-25 T10: zen bed moved from the reserved (11,3) to (20,2) r4.3 — sun is on the −z side all day (sky.js az path), so (11,3) sat in the two sakura trees' shadows at t9/t15 (bed read dark blue 50-96 vs grass 74-130 in sun); (20,2) is clear of every shadow cone at both review times, projects mid-right in the overview frame, and is clear of all 8 boulders, path, pond and bamboo.
- 2026-11-25 T10: overview rake readability — texture-only ridges (any SP ≤ 0.5u) are annihilated by mipmap minification at the 88px bed (A/B in-page: no-mip row sd 62 with ±100 oscillations vs mip row sd 33 flat). Fixed with GEOMETRIC macro ridges: bed fan vertices displaced ±0.045u at SP_G 1.0u (same axis + stone circles as texture, rim-faded); real relief, immune to mips, ~10px/ridge at overview (verified: clean gravel row oscillates ±35 levels, ~10-sample period). Texture SP set 0.5u so its crests align with every 2nd macro crest; axis rotated to world z (screen-vertical) since the overview foreshortens z.
- 2026-11-25 T10: gravel albedo dropped 214→176 and groove shade widened to 0.64+0.62g — the old pale albedo sat in the ACES shoulder where contrast was crushed; bed now reads 148±54 in sun.
- 2026-11-25 T11: petal sprite texture now fills the WHOLE 64×64 canvas (previously ~45% coverage with transparent margins) — at overview distance the 0.16u quads are ~2px and sample the highest mips, whose alpha average was < alphaTest 0.5, so EVERY petal was silently discarded (verified A/B in-page: alphaTest on = 0 px, off = 1764 px). Same mip-annihilation family as T10 rake lines. Also affects T08 ground petals (they now fill their quads more — same look class).
- 2026-11-25 T11: falling petals settle on `island.terrainHeight()` + analytic pond test (d < waterR(θ)·0.97 → rest at waterY) instead of per-frame raycasts — the raw height is exact outside the in-place-edited zones (T09 decision), and the only zone that matters for petal resting (the pond) is handled analytically; 3000 petals stay at 60fps.
- 2026-11-25 T11: fireflies are ONE THREE.Points (40, additive, glow sprite, per-vertex colour = blink, opacity = smoothstep((night−0.3)/0.4)) — no extra lights (cap 5 of 7 stays), one draw call; size 1.5u (~11px at overview) because 0.55u cores measured <80 ΔR and read as nothing.
- 2026-11-25 T12: post chain = RenderPass→GTAO→UnrealBloom→SMAA→vignette→OutputPass in src/post.js; OutputPass reads renderer.toneMapping/exposure so ACES+sRGB happen once (renderer tone-maps only when rendering to screen; RTs stay linear — verified: post=0 output is byte-identical to pre-T12 baseline, lum 0.661). Bloom THRESHOLD is modulated by night factor (8.2 day → 0.8 night, setNight every frame): measured with tools/probe-sky.mjs (renders scene to a Float RT + readPixels) that day-sky linear luma is median 4.5 / p90 6.4 / max 7.5 while night lantern lamps are ~2.3–3.9 and fireflies ~1.0 — no static threshold can keep day sky from blooming while letting night glow bloom. A fixed 0.12 strength still whitened the sky (brightPct 37%); threshold-gating gives day = baseline (brightPct 0) and night meanLum 0.073→0.094 (glow lift).
- 2026-11-25 T12: renderer.info.autoReset=false + info.reset() per frame in animate — EffectComposer renders ~6 passes/frame, so autoReset left __diag.drawCalls at 1 (final pass only); now 284 (all passes incl. GTAO prepass), still << 600.
- 2026-11-25 T12: visible-diff verification done with screenshot A/B + numpy/PIL pixel diff (tools/ab-diff.py, tools/ab-bloom.py) instead of in-page readPixels — the composer writes the canvas (no preserveDrawingBuffer), so backbuffer probes can't isolate post effects; PNG A/B (post=0 vs post=1, same seed/time/preset) is the direct measure of "differs visibly". Pass criteria met without parameter changes: night lantern halo ring-lift +23/+26 (handoff floor was 15), vignette corners up to −27 levels (floor 5).
- 2026-11-25 T13: live vs reload split for the controls panel — time/pause/speed/petals/wind/toggles (auto-rotate, shadows, post, fireflies, water, wireframe)/presets/reset are all LIVE (drive the shared `state` in main.js or a one-line system call); **quality and seed-regenerate reload the page** (rewrite URL param + location.search=). Reasoning: quality/seed are baked into every system at build time (terrain/pagoda/tree/grass instance counts, shadow map size, post chain scale, firefly count — 10+ sites), so a true live rebuild = rebuilding the whole scene, while a reload is trivially correct and deterministic. Panel is still the single entry point for both; documented in the handoff.
- 2026-11-25 T13: falling petals are now ALWAYS allocated at MAX=3000 and the visible count is `InstancedMesh.count` (setCount() from the panel), mesh.visible=false at 0 — one-time allocation makes the petal slider a free live control; per-frame update loops over mesh.count only, so low counts cost nothing (verified 60fps, draw calls unchanged at 284).
- 2026-11-25 T13: post-processing chain is ALWAYS built in src/post.js; the toggle switches render() between composer.render() and plain renderer.render() — byte-identical plain path (T12 invariant) at the cost of the composer's RTs being allocated even with post=0 (few extra textures, negligible).
- 2026-11-25 T13: T13 control verification method (for the T14 reuse): tools/probe-sky.mjs + one async expression that drives each control via DOM (set value + dispatchEvent input/change, click, KeyboardEvent) and reads window.__diag — MUST `await wait(60)` (≥1 rAF) after every action, because __diag is only rewritten during the animate loop (synchronous reads give last-frame values — hit this first probe run, every control looked broken). New __diag fields make assertions trivial: paused, cycleSpeed, wind, autoRotate, shadows, fireflies, water, wireframe. Reload-type controls assert via CDP error "Inspected target navigated or closed".
- 2026-11-25 T14: tools/check-controls.mjs (new tool; shot.mjs untouched) drives all controls over CDP and asserts __diag. Two implementation notes: (1) test fns are serialized via fn.toString() to the page, so a test body may only reference its own params/locals — loop variables (e.g. the preset key "1".."5") must be passed as JSON args, not closed over. (2) reload detection: a search-only URL change navigates the document, but Runtime.evaluate of a trivial expr in the fresh context just resolves (no "navigated" error) — so we poll location.search for the new param value, then poll __diag until the rebuilt scene reports it.
- 2026-11-25 T15: time-of-day exposure in sky.js (renderer.toneMappingExposure, applied per frame in main.js): base 0.38→0.305 (day views were hot: pagoda brightPct 21, torii 15.8 — both > the 10 strict line), +0.22·sunsetF (sunset island was a backlit silhouette: darkPct 26.1 > 25), +0.3·nightF (night was near pitch black: meanLum 0.094, darkPct 67.6). Exposure is applied at OutputPass (post=1) and by the renderer (post=0) identically; bloom threshold is on PRE-tonemap linear luma, so it is unaffected (T12 gate stays valid).
- 2026-11-25 T15: sunset sun/hemi boosts (sun +1.9·sunsetF·dayF, hemi +0.8·sunsetF, hemi sunset-tint lerp 0.35→0.5) because at 18.5 the sun is 2.4° above the horizon (dot with up-facing terrain ≈ 0.04) — the island only gets hemi light on the camera side, so hemi is the real lever, not the sun.
- 2026-11-25 T15: night lift = moonLight 3.2→5.5, hemi night floor +0.35·nightF, C_FOG_NIGHT 0x1e2c4a→0x2a3d63 (haze dome + fog), exposure +0.3·nightF. Kept night dark (darkPct 47, mostly true sky above horizon) — a moonlit night at luma 0.148 with a legible island (lower-frame edge 13.9, col 355) rather than "dim day"; the rubric says no pitch black, and the black remaining is physically-correct upper sky with stars.
- 2026-11-25 T15: new 6th light: cool cloud-bounce DirectionalLight (0x9db4d8, 0.42+0.2·dayF, from (20,−130,26), no shadows) — the underside cone only faces down, so sun and hemisphere give it ZERO normal-dependent shading (hemisphere ignores normals), which is why the rock read as one flat grey regardless of texture. A directional from below restores normal-map relief (verified: rock-region edge%(>12) 1.3→5.9). Lights 5→6, still ≤7; no T14 check asserts light count.
- 2026-11-25 T15: underside rock realism (was colours4bit 58, meanSat 0.073, no hues): (a) per-stratum vertex colour now ±~40% luminance contrast + rusty/cool hue split (rusty = smoothstep(band hash)), (b) strata texture got per-band blue/ruddy tints, 2-octave fine mottle (freq 5.1→9.2), deeper streaks, (c) height-map mottle 0.25→0.5 + normalFromHeightCanvas strength 1.8→3.0 + material normalScale →2.4, (d) strata UV tiled u×3 (texture is u-periodic via noise3D on a circle) so the 512×256 map stops upscaling over the 90u cone. Result: edgePct 3.7→15.4, red hue 13%, rock-region edges 4×.
- 2026-11-25 T15: day sky turbidity 6+5(1−dayF) → 10+5(1−dayF) — the physical sky's zenith is what drove pagoda/torii brightPct >10 after exposure cuts alone (sky is 17-22% of the pagoda frame, nearly all >0.96 luma). Turbidity 10 softens the zenith to luma ≤0.96 (pagoda brightPct 6.6, torii 1.8) and adds sky-gradient detail (edgePct up), at the cost of a slightly hazier sky — a realistic trade.
