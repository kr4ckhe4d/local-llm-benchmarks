The check request (Anthropic endpoint, same glob prompt) passed glob 8/8 in 3,838 tokens, so the
model can solve these; at temp 0 it mostly talks itself out of the token budget instead. Thinking
on was not run: at ~20 tok/s a thinking run at GLM's lengths would take hours.

Speed: 19.9 tok/s generation (700-token probe, n=2), load 30 s. --fit puts ~49 GB on the two cards
and ~14 GB of experts in RAM (RSS 14.8 GB), so nothing streams from disk.

Getting it to run on upstream llama.cpp (conversion/translate.py; the download targets the
lighttransport Strata fork):
1. names: 3 hyper-connection keys, kda gate bound, swiglu clamp; 294 tensors (hc_* + .weight,
   indexer.kpool_* -> indexer_compressor_*.weight); indexer kpool = 4 from zai-org config.json.
2. keys the fork never wrote, copied from unsloth's GLM-5.3-Flash shard 1 (upstream converter):
   attention.value_length 512 (fork: 0), eot/eom/bos/unk token ids, layer_norm_epsilon,
   expert_shared_feed_forward_length, indexer.index_share_mtp.
3. ssm_a sign: the fork stores +exp(A_log), upstream -exp(A_log). With 1+2 only, output was
   "Understood. I will not use the term ..." forever. Found by range-fetching small F32 tensors from
   unsloth's shard 2 and comparing: every other compared tensor was bit-identical.
Load: dio, not mmap -- mmap thrashed the 30 GB page cache and stalled 9+ min; dio loads in 30 s.
n-gpu-layers = auto in the preset, or --fit aborts on [*]'s 99.
reasoning_effort low: the template otherwise injects "Reasoning Effort: Max"; with thinking cut off
the model then reasons in the answer and, at temp 0, loops (conversion/sample-effort-max-loop.txt).
